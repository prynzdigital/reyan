    The Mission

    Faith that goes, gives and serves

    We believe the Gospel is good news for the whole person. Our mission work brings the Word, compassion and practical help to communities that need it most.

    Gospel Crusades

    Open-air crusades and revival meetings in towns and villages, preaching salvation, healing and hope.

    Leadership Training

    Equipping pastors and young leaders with sound doctrine, practical ministry skills and resources.

    Richard Engmann Preacher · Teacher · Missionary

    About Richard Engmann

    A voice for the Gospel, a heart for the nations

    Richard Engmann answered the call to preach as a young man and has given his life to proclaiming Jesus Christ with clarity, passion and compassion. His ministry carries a simple burden: that every person would hear the Gospel and come to know the love of the Father.

    Through local churches, open-air crusades, conferences and online platforms, Richard teaches the Word of God in a way that is deep yet simple — calling believers to prayer, holiness and bold witness.

    Our Vision

    To see lives transformed, leaders raised and nations reached through the power of the Gospel.

    Our Values

    The Word. Prayer. Integrity. Compassion. Excellence. Souls.
